Technical & Creative Writing

Overview

I write to make a decision easier for the next reader. Technical writing names the system, the constraint, and the next action. Creative writing names the stake and the feeling without lying about the mechanics. I use both on purpose: a guide that cannot be followed is decoration, and a pitch that cannot be checked is advertising.

Core Strengths

  • Turning a messy process into a page someone else can run
  • Writing for two audiences on one artifact: operator and outsider
  • Keeping tone consistent across docs, site copy, and live scripts
  • Cutting lore when the reader needs a rule; adding scene when the reader needs a reason to care
  • Revising from use: if people still ask the same question, the page is wrong

Relevant Experience

  • Design and process docs — Game and campaign design documents (structure, economy, turn order, open questions). Written so a builder can implement and a stakeholder can see what is not decided yet.
  • Training guides — Pre-Selection and tutoring write-ups: decision tools, note-taking under load, self-management, social skill broken into drills. The test is whether a reader can act without sitting in voice chat.
  • Professional site and competency map — Public pages that state what I do, with what evidence, in a format a hiring manager can scan.
  • Scripts and pitches — Live-sale and event copy: one claim, three objections, a close. Short enough to say on camera.
  • Operational writing — Rules, role blurbs, verification notes, and handoff docs so a community or team does not live in my DMs.

Approach

  • Start from the job of the page: instruct, persuade, record, or hand off — pick one primary job
  • Put the rule before the story. Put the story only where motivation or context is the blocker
  • Name owners, inputs, and “done.” Vague writing creates hidden work
  • Use the same words the team already uses. New vocabulary is a cost
  • Ship a draft that can be wrong in public. Fix from the questions it produces

What This Means for Clients / Collaborators

I can produce the document the work is waiting on: a spec, a lesson, a public explainer, or a short script. The standard is not style. It is that a competent stranger can use the page without a side conversation with me.
