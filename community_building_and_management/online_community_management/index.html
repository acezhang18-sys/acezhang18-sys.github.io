Online Community Management

Overview

I build and run online groups that have a purpose, a standard, and a next person who can hold the keys. I work in Discord and the tools around it. I optimize for structure, not audience size.

Core Strengths

  • Stand up roles, channels, and rules so the group is usable on day one
  • Set a purpose tight enough that random traffic does not become the culture
  • Recruit and brief moderators instead of being the only adult in the room
  • Hand off ownership on purpose so the community is not a personality
  • Keep civilian and specialist spaces distinct without turning the place into a clique

Relevant Experience

  • Pre-Selection — Founded a Discord community for SOF preparation. Defined purpose, staff roles, and how military claims get handled. Built a leadership bench, then transferred server ownership so the group could run without the founder as the choke point.
  • 38th Tactical Division — Organized a large cooperative unit with rank, staff chat, and a command handoff (Colonel / Lieutenant Colonel structure). Same pattern: stand it up, staff it, give the seat away when the org can hold itself.
  • Team Echoes — Ran a remote product community around a game studio, including a Player Design Committee with elected input so design talk had a channel that was not mob rule and not a silent founder veto.
  • Day-to-day ops — Channel design, staff onboarding, verification of who is speaking as what, and keeping general chat from eating the mission.

Method

  • Purpose first, then rooms. If a channel has no job, it becomes noise.
  • Write the few rules people will actually enforce. Unenforced rules train contempt.
  • Staff is a team, not an audience with icons. Brief them. Let them decide the small calls.
  • Founder stays visible enough to set tone, quiet enough that the place works when they are offline.
  • Exit is part of the build. If ownership cannot move, it was a personal brand, not a community.

What This Means in Practice

Can take a loose online crowd and leave a staffed space with a stated job, a bench, and a clean handoff. Useful when a company, unit, or project needs a living room that does not collapse into the loudest voice.
