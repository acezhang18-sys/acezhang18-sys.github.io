Team Leadership

Overview

I specialize in building and leading small-to-medium teams in high-stakes, high-autonomy environments. My approach centers on clear standards, deliberate culture-building, and developing people who can operate independently.

Core Strengths

  • Establishing direction and standards from the ground up
  • Developing others into capable leaders
  • Maintaining cohesion under pressure or ambiguity
  • Making hard calls while preserving long-term team health
  • Creating systems that outlast any single person

Relevant Experience

  • Founded and led Pre-Selection, a specialized Discord community focused on Special Forces preparation. Built the culture, structure, and leadership pipeline from scratch, then deliberately transitioned ownership to develop the next leader.
  • Held leadership roles in organized gaming communities (including the 38th Tactical Division), managing team structure, standards, and performance in competitive environments.
  • Mentored multiple individuals into greater responsibility and independence, focusing on both skill development and decision-making maturity.

Leadership Philosophy

  • Standards over motivation
  • Clarity over control
  • Develop people who can lead without you
  • Protect the long-term health of the group, even when it requires difficult short-term decisions

What This Means for Clients / Collaborators

I can step into a team that needs structure, raise the standard of execution, develop existing members, and leave the group stronger and more self-sufficient than I found it.
