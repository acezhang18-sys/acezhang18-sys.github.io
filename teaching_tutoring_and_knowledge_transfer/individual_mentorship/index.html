Individual Mentorship

Overview

I treat one-to-one work as diagnostic tutoring plus leadership apprenticeship. First isolate what is breaking performance. Then train the real constraints. For leadership, I add example, progressive handoff, and ownership without blame so the person learns to run the room, not just copy me.

Core Method

  • Run simple assessments that separate knowledge gaps, pressure failures, and self-sabotaging patterns
  • Name the root constraint and attack it in focused sessions
  • Lead by visible example and explain the non-obvious intent behind key moves
  • Hand real problems over in stages so judgment develops under live conditions
  • Praise hard-to-see wins, correct without taking the wheel, and stay present after failure without piling on blame

Relevant Experience

  • ASVAB and accession prep — Diagnostic sessions built from scores and practice data. Weak sections, timing collapse, and repeatable mistakes were isolated and trained separately.
  • Pre-Selection — One-to-one guidance inside a preparation community: diagnosis of plan, consistency, and ego blockers, plus leadership practice by letting people take real staff and floor problems while I stayed available as anchor, not as replacement.
  • 38th and similar units — Mentees shadowed how standards, ownership, and de-escalation actually looked in motion. Hidden purpose of quiet moves was explained after the fact so observation turned into transferable judgment.

Method

Diagnosis
  • Assess first with short tasks that surface knowledge gaps, execution under load, or repeatable self-interference.
  • Isolate root causes. One primary constraint per focused block of work.
  • Hand back a plain map: what is blocking you, what to practice, how you will know it moved.

Leadership Apprenticeship
  • Leadership by example: they watch the ordinary runs. When a move is not obvious, I name the hidden purpose so they see the system, not just the surface action.
  • Progressive handoff: introduce live problems from the organization in increasing difficulty. They propose and run the solution. I do not pre-solve it for them.
  • Ownership, not blame: call out positive results that are easy to miss and often counter-intuitive. On misses, point at the signal, suggest a course, and leave the decision with them. When results go bad, stay present, do not pile on, and treat the harsh outcome as the teacher.

Exit
  • Reduce dependence: The win is their diagnosis and their judgment the next time the room gets loud.
  • Hand off ownership: The organization itself is the end goal and the solution, not my personal brand nor ego.
  • Offer new opportunity: Pass down the opportunity of leadership to apprentices to spread knowledge and experience as well as grant the organization fresh new perspectives.

What This Means for Clients / Collaborators

I can take someone stuck on a skill or on early leadership, run a clean diagnosis, train the real constraints, and grow them through example and live handoff until they can see their own blockers and own the room without me in it. Built for test prep, skill plateaus, and developing leaders inside real groups.
